READABILITY REVIEW

Author of Review: Sami Mannan
Date of Review: 11-16-2026
Sprint Number: 4
Files Being Reviewed: BarrelStorm.cs, Camera2D.cs
Author of File(s): Meera (BarrelStorm), Arav(Camera2D)
Time Elapsed: 25 minutes

Notes:
BarrelStorm is very long, mostly because of the other classes that are stored in this file. The variables are distinct and clear, with each one having a descriptive enough 
name that they don't become easily confused. Some of the method names could be clearer- CancelRoomEffect and Reset don't have enough of a distinction on their own to justify 
being two separate methods instead of the same one, despite having different uses. Camera2D is very short and clear in what it accomplishes. All the method names are clear 
distinct, not allowing for any logical overlap. Likewise, the variables are used and named well.

Improvements:
BarrelStorm, BarrelProjectile, and DustParticle should be separated. This should greatly improve readability of all three classes, since the file won't be as long and 
everything will look more organized. There's almost excessive overuse of separating parameters on different lines, which is fine for method calls but makes the lines assigning 
values to variables look needlessly messy. I can't think of much for Camera2D, it's already very coherent.
